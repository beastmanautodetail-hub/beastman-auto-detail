THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Mini Exterior + Full Interior

Pair a light exterior refresh with a comprehensive interior detail.

What it entails

  • MINI EXTERIOR: wheel/tire cleaning + tire shine, bug removal, snow foam hand wash, exterior windows
  • FULL INTERIOR: full vacuum, steam cleaning, scrubbed hard surfaces, seat/leather cleaning, floor mats, crevices, door jambs, interior windows

Specialized interior treatments such as carpet extraction can be selected separately where needed.

When to choose it

This fits a vehicle whose cabin sees more wear than its exterior. Examples include crumbs between seats, dirty mats and marks around cupholders while the outside only needs a routine wash.

The bundle directs the more comprehensive cleaning effort toward the cabin, while still refreshing the outside in the same visit. It can be a sensible choice after family travel or everyday commuting when the interior has fallen behind the exterior in upkeep.

If the cabin only needs light cleaning, the Mini bundle may be enough. If the exterior also needs the sealant, trim protection and wheel-well care included in Full Exterior, consider the Full bundle. Concentrated stains or heavy hair should still be discussed as separate concerns.

Why choose this over another option?

This directs more of the appointment to the interior. Choose the Mini bundle for light cleaning throughout, or the Full bundle for deeper exterior care too.

  • Compare Mini Interior + Exterior

  • Compare Full Interior + Exterior
