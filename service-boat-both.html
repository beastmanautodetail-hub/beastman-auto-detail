Boat Interior + Exterior Detail

Boat pricing is based on linear feet. Final duration is confirmed based on size and condition.

What it entails

  • Review vehicle length, condition and access
  • Agree the areas to be cleaned and the service scope
  • Confirm the estimate and expected appointment time

Describe the vehicle, length, materials and condition. The exact cleaning tasks and appointment setup must be confirmed before work begins.

When to choose it

Choose this when both interior and exterior areas of your boat need attention and you want to discuss the complete scope for one visit. Make a short list of the concerns inside and outside so neither side is overlooked.

Combined service is useful when both areas genuinely need work. If only the interior or exterior needs cleaning, a single-area option may be more appropriate. The included tasks must still be agreed for the vehicle, its materials and its condition.

Provide the length, location, access arrangements and any concentrated stains or heavy buildup. These details help establish the estimate and expected time. Confirm them directly with Matt because the current booking does not automatically calculate a final length-based total.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose the interior and exterior option to match the areas you want cleaned. Choose the combined service when both areas need attention.
