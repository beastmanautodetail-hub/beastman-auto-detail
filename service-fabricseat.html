Fabric Seat Shampoo & Heated Extraction

Deep-cleans fabric seats with shampoo and heated extraction to lift dirt, odors, and embedded grime.

What it entails

  • Deep-cleans fabric seats with shampoo and heated extraction to lift dirt, odors, and embedded grime.

Select the number of seats to treat and describe any stains. Allow for any drying guidance given after cleaning.

When to choose it

Choose this for fabric seats with visible soil, spill marks or staining that needs attention beyond the package seat cleaning. Be specific about which seats and which areas are affected so you select the right quantity.

This is useful when the concern is in the upholstery rather than only loose crumbs between seats. Tell Matt about previous attempts to clean the fabric and any recurring marks so expectations can be discussed.

Fabric wear, fading and damaged material cannot be treated as ordinary dirt. Results vary with the stain and condition, and drying needs should be planned for. Choose leather cleaning and conditioning for leather upholstery rather than selecting a fabric treatment.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose this for fabric upholstery. Leather Seat Cleaning & Conditioning is the material-specific alternative for leather seats.

  • Compare Leather Seat Cleaning & Conditioning

    Choose leather cleaning and conditioning for leather seats. Choose fabric shampoo and extraction for fabric upholstery; the material determines the suitable option.

    Choose Full Interior for cleaning throughout the cabin. Add fabric seat extraction when the seats need more focused work than the package seat cleaning.
