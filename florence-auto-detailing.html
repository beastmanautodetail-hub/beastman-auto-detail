LOCAL QUESTIONS

Before your Florence detail.

Can you come to my Florence property outside downtown?

Florence is in our service area. Send the exact address and access details so we can discuss the location and scheduling before you plan the visit.

What if my driveway has a gravel approach?

Tell us in advance. We need to discuss the actual working surface, room around the vehicle, and a suitable setup rather than assuming every approach will work.

Will an exterior detail fix faded paint on a parked vehicle?

Cleaning and protection are different from restoring damaged paint. We can discuss an assessment for paint work, but failing clear coat or permanent damage will not be fixed by a wash.
