Wheel Well Cleaning

Removes built-up mud, road grime, salt, and debris from wheel wells for a cleaner, more finished appearance.

What it entails

  • Removes built-up mud, road grime, salt, and debris from wheel wells for a cleaner, more finished appearance.

Flag heavy buildup when booking so expectations can be confirmed after inspection.

When to choose it

Look inside the wheel arches rather than only at the wheel faces. Visible mud or road grime in those spaces may remain a concern even when the tires and wheels look clean.

This add-on fits someone choosing a lighter exterior package who also wants focused attention inside the arches. Tell Matt about substantial mud or debris before the appointment so the cleanup can be assessed.

Full Exterior already includes wheel-well care. Select the add-on only where it adds work that is not already in your package. This is cleaning, not repair of damaged liners, rust or other components.

Why choose this over another option?

This targets wheel wells rather than just wheels and tires. Full Exterior already includes wheel-well care, so booking hides the duplicate add-on for those packages.
