UTV Exterior Detail

An exterior clean for your side-by-side or UTV.

What it entails

  • Wheel and tire cleaning / tire shine
  • Pre-wash
  • Bug removal
  • Snow foam hand wash
  • Exterior window cleaning

Describe accumulated mud and the vehicle condition before booking.

When to choose it

This is a fit when your UTV or side-by-side needs exterior cleanup but the cabin can be left alone. Describe dirt around the bodywork, wheels and other outside surfaces rather than judging only the most visible panel.

After an outing, check the cabin as well. If seats, floors and crevices also need attention, the Interior + Exterior option may be a better match than choosing exterior-only and expecting interior work.

Mention heavy mud, accessories and any damaged areas before booking. The listed service is a cleaning package; unusual buildup or extra work needs review. Both UTV options currently list the same estimated duration, so compare the included work and price rather than choosing by time alone.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose UTV Interior + Exterior when the cabin also needs vacuuming, surface cleaning and seat attention.
