FAQ

Before you book.

What if my vehicle is too dirty for a Mini Detail?

Mini services are intended for vehicles in generally good condition requiring light cleaning. If the condition is beyond the package scope, we may recommend a Full Detail or additional service.

Can I select multiple add-ons?

Yes. The custom booking system lets you add multiple services, and your total price and estimated appointment time update as you build your detail.

How are Paint Correction and Ceramic Coating booked?

Those services require an estimate because pricing and timing depend on paint condition and the desired result.

Do you service large vehicles?

Yes. RVs, trailers, commercial trucks, boats, motorcycles, and UTVs have their own service flow and pricing rules.
