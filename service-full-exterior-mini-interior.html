THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Full Exterior + Mini Interior

Pair a deeper protected exterior detail with a light interior reset.

What it entails

  • FULL EXTERIOR: wheel/tire cleaning + dressing, wheel wells, bug/tar removal, snow foam hand wash, exterior windows, 3-month sealant, UV dressing
  • MINI INTERIOR: light vacuum, interior windows, hard-surface wipe-down, light seat/leather cleaning, light door-jamb wipe-down

Exterior protection does not include paint correction or ceramic coating.

When to choose it

Choose this when the exterior needs the fuller care package but the cabin is still generally tidy. For example, you may want wheel-well cleaning and sealant while only needing a light vacuum, glass cleaning and interior wipe-down.

This combination avoids choosing a comprehensive interior reset simply to get the Full Exterior service. It suits drivers who keep the cabin maintained but want the added exterior care and protection in this package.

If seats, mats and crevices have accumulated substantial dirt, the Full bundle may fit better. If light washing is all the exterior needs, consider the Mini bundle. Paint defects or persistent water spots still need their own assessment rather than being assumed part of the exterior package.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose this over the Full bundle when a comprehensive interior clean is unnecessary. Choose the Mini bundle if a maintenance wash is enough outside.
