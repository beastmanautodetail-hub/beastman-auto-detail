Leather Seat Cleaning & Conditioning

Gently removes dirt, body oils, and buildup, then conditions leather to help protect against drying and cracking.

What it entails

  • Gently removes dirt, body oils, and buildup, then conditions leather to help protect against drying and cracking.

Select the number of seats and point out existing wear or damage before treatment.

When to choose it

Consider this when leather seats need focused cleaning and conditioning beyond light upkeep. Look for accumulated grime on frequently used areas and describe any sticky or dirty patches you want reviewed.

Select the number of seats that need attention rather than assuming the whole cabin needs the same treatment. Tell Matt about the upholstery type if known and any previous products or treatments used on it.

Cracking, peeling, tears and worn color are different from surface dirt. This service is not leather repair or recoloring. If the seats only need light maintenance, an appropriate interior package may be sufficient; fabric upholstery needs its own material-appropriate option.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose this for leather rather than the fabric shampoo service. It adds targeted attention beyond the package seat cleaning.

  • Compare Fabric Seat Shampoo & Heated Extraction

    Choose fabric shampoo and extraction for fabric upholstery. Leather cleaning and conditioning is the material-specific option for leather seats.

  • Compare Mini Interior Detail

    Choose Mini Interior when light seat cleaning and general cabin upkeep are sufficient. Add this per-seat service when leather needs focused cleaning and conditioning.
