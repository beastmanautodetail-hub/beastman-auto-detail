When to choose it

Consider this when you want the 5-year coating option together with its included 2-step paint correction. It is intended for a customer choosing more intensive preparation as part of the coating package.

Discuss the defects you can see, any repainting or existing coating, and the finish you hope to achieve. Preparing the paint and protecting the resulting finish are separate parts of the decision; the coating itself should not be expected to erase imperfections.

If lighter enhancement fits your goals, the 3-year package may be more appropriate. If you only want paint restoration, compare standalone correction. Inspection, pricing, appointment timing and aftercare expectations should be confirmed before committing to the work.

Why choose this over another option?

This includes 2-step paint correction. The 3-year package includes a paint enhancement instead, making it a different preparation and protection choice.
