UTV Interior + Exterior Detail

A complete inside-and-out detail for your side-by-side.

What it entails

  • Exterior wash and cleaning
  • Full vacuum
  • Interior glass cleaning
  • Hard surfaces scrubbed and cleaned
  • Seat cleaning
  • Crevice cleaning and blow-out

Empty loose gear and describe the level of dirt before the appointment.

When to choose it

Choose this when dirt from use has reached both the outside of the UTV and the cabin. Look at seats, floors, crevices and glass as well as exterior panels and wheels.

The combined package adds the listed cabin cleaning to the exterior work. It is a practical choice when you want the whole vehicle refreshed instead of leaving a dirty interior after the outside is washed.

Remove loose gear and describe substantial mud or concentrated dirt before the visit. If only the outside needs attention, Exterior is the lower-priced option. The two options currently share a listed time estimate, but they include different work.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose UTV Exterior when only the outside needs cleaning. This package adds cabin surfaces, seats, glass and crevices.
