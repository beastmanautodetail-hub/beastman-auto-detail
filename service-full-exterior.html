THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Full Exterior Detail

A deeper exterior clean with added paint and trim protection.

What it entails

  • Wheel and tire cleaning / dressing
  • Wheel well cleaning / dressing
  • Bug and tar removal
  • Snow foam hand wash
  • Exterior window cleaning
  • 3-month paint protection sealant
  • Exterior plastics dressed with UV protection

The included protection is different from correcting scratches or removing etched water spots.

When to choose it

Choose this when you want the exterior cleaned and its care extended beyond a maintenance wash. Dirt inside wheel arches, exterior trim needing attention and a desire for the included sealant are reasons to consider the Full option.

It is a useful choice when bringing a vehicle back into a regular care routine or when you want the included wash, wheel-well cleaning and protection in one appointment. If the exterior only has light dust and you do not need those extras, Mini Exterior may be sufficient.

Protection and correction are different goals. The included sealant does not make scratches disappear, and washing does not guarantee removal of persistent mineral spots. Describe paint defects or stubborn deposits so Matt can explain whether a targeted add-on is appropriate.

Why choose this over another option?

Mini Exterior is the maintenance wash option. Full Exterior adds wheel-well cleaning, a 3-month sealant and UV dressing; paint correction and ceramic coatings are separate options.

  • Compare Mini Exterior Detail

    Choose Mini Exterior for routine light cleaning when you do not need the added protection and wheel-well care. Full Exterior is worth choosing when you want those extras together.

  • Compare Paint Enhancement

    Add Paint Enhancement if dullness and light swirl marks are your concern. Full Exterior cleans and protects; it does not include machine polishing.
