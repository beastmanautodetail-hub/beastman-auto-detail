THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Paint Correction

Machine polishing used to remove or significantly reduce deeper swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and other paint imperfections. Pricing and service time are determined after inspecting the vehicle’s paint condition.

What it entails

  • Machine polishing used to remove or significantly reduce deeper swirl marks, scratches, oxidation, and other paint imperfections. Pricing and service time are determined after inspecting the vehicle’s paint condition.

An inspection determines the price and service time. No fixed price or duration is promised.

When to choose it

Choose an assessment when visible swirl marks, scratches, oxidation or other imperfections remain after washing and a light gloss improvement may not meet your goal. Explain which defects you hope to improve and how much improvement matters to you.

Paint condition varies between vehicles and even between panels. Previous repairs, repainting and known finish damage are useful details to share. The purpose of the inspection is to decide a suitable scope rather than promise a fixed correction result from a description alone.

If the goal is a lighter overall gloss improvement, Paint Enhancement may be enough. If correction is part of a coating plan, compare the preparation included with the 5-year ceramic package. Price and timing are confirmed with the estimate.

Why choose this over another option?

Paint Enhancement is the lighter gloss-focused option. Paint Correction is selected when the paint needs more involved restoration; ceramic packages combine their specified preparation with coating.

  • Compare Paint Enhancement

    Choose Paint Enhancement when a lighter gloss improvement is enough. Correction is for paint that needs more involved assessment and restoration, with pricing and timing confirmed after inspection.

  • Compare 5-Year Ceramic Coating + 2-Step Paint Correction

    Choose the 5-year ceramic package if you also want its coating after the included 2-step correction. Standalone correction suits someone choosing paint restoration without that coating package.
