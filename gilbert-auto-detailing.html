LOCAL QUESTIONS

Before your Gilbert detail.

What should I do with child seats before a Gilbert appointment?

If you want the area underneath cleaned, remove the seats before service and reinstall them yourself afterward. We can discuss access to the surrounding upholstery in advance.

Will an interior detail remove every old spill?

Results depend on the material and how long the spill has been there. Tell us about it before booking so we can discuss treatment options without promising complete removal.

Can I book interior and exterior care together in Gilbert?

Yes. Choose Both in the existing booking flow, then compare the Mini and Full options for your vehicle type and condition.
