THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Paint Enhancement

A light polishing service designed to improve gloss and overall appearance. Helps reduce the look of minor swirl marks, light scratches, and dullness.

What it entails

  • A light polishing service designed to improve gloss and overall appearance. Helps reduce the look of minor swirl marks, light scratches, and dullness.

This is not a promise to remove every scratch. We assess the paint before deciding the appropriate scope.

When to choose it

Consider this when washed paint still looks a little dull or shows light swirl marks and you want a glossier overall appearance. It is intended as a light polishing service, not a promise of complete defect removal.

Describe what bothers you most: general appearance, small swirls or a few deeper marks. The difference matters because choosing a more intensive treatment based only on the word polishing may mean selecting work the paint does not need.

Deeper scratches, oxidation or more involved defects should be assessed for Paint Correction. If you also want the 3-year ceramic package, enhancement is already part of that option. Final preparation should be matched to the paint condition and your goals.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose Paint Enhancement for a light polishing service. Paint Correction is assessed separately for deeper imperfections; the 3-year ceramic package already includes enhancement.

  • Compare Paint Correction

    Choose Paint Correction when deeper scratches, oxidation or heavier defects need an inspection. Enhancement is the lighter polishing choice for gloss and minor imperfections.

  • Compare 3-Year Ceramic Coating + Paint Enhancement

    Choose the 3-year ceramic package if you want a coating as well as enhancement. It already includes enhancement, so discuss the complete package rather than duplicating that work.
