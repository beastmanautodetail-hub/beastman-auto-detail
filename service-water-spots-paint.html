THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Water Spot Removal - Paint

Targets water spots and mineral deposits on painted exterior surfaces.

What it entails

  • Targets water spots and mineral deposits on painted exterior surfaces.

Describe the affected panels. Deeply etched marks may need a separate assessment; complete removal is not guaranteed.

When to choose it

Look for pale rings, speckled marks or patches that remain on painted panels after normal washing. Tell Matt whether the vehicle has been exposed to sprinklers, repeated splashes or water drying on the surface, and how long you have noticed the marks.

This add-on focuses on spotting rather than general dirt or overall dullness. Describe which panels are affected and whether the marks cover a small area or much of the vehicle. That information helps distinguish a localized concern from broader paint restoration.

Not every visible spot has the same cause or depth. Some marks may require a different approach after inspection, and complete removal is not guaranteed. Choose the window-specific treatment for glass; discuss Paint Enhancement or Paint Correction if scratches or widespread paint defects are also a concern.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose this for spotting on painted surfaces. Water Spot Removal - Windows targets glass instead; Paint Enhancement addresses overall gloss and light defects.

  • Compare Water Spot Removal - Windows

    Choose the window treatment when the spots are on glass. Pick the paint treatment for painted panels; if both surfaces are affected, they are separate add-ons.

  • Compare Paint Enhancement

    Choose Paint Enhancement when the main concern is overall gloss, dullness or light swirl marks. Choose water-spot removal when visible mineral spotting is the specific problem to assess.
