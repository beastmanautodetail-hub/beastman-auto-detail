Carpet Floor Mat Deep Cleaning

Shampoos carpet floor mats and uses heated extraction to lift dirt, odors, embedded grime, and common stains.

What it entails

  • Shampoos carpet floor mats and uses heated extraction to lift dirt, odors, embedded grime, and common stains.

Select the number of carpet mats. Discuss drying before placing freshly cleaned mats back into use.

When to choose it

This is a focused choice when removable carpet mats have embedded soil or spill marks that remain a concern after ordinary cleaning. Check whether the issue is in the mat itself or has also reached the fitted carpet underneath.

The service is priced per carpet mat, so choose the number affected. It can be more appropriate than cabin carpet extraction when the rest of the floor is clean and only the removable mats need deeper treatment.

Tell Matt about old stains, worn areas and any treatments already tried. Cleaning cannot restore missing or worn carpet fibers, and stain removal is not guaranteed. Ask about drying before putting the cleaned mats back into regular use.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose this for individual carpet mats. Carpet Shampoo & Heated Extraction addresses the cabin carpets instead.
