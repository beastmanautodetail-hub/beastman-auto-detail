THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Mini Exterior Detail

A light exterior refresh for vehicles in generally good condition.

What it entails

  • Wheel and tire cleaning / tire shine
  • Bug removal
  • Snow foam hand wash
  • Exterior window cleaning

Flag heavy mud or stubborn deposits before the appointment so the service matches the condition.

When to choose it

This fits a vehicle with light everyday dust, road film, fingerprints on exterior glass and ordinary wheel and tire dirt. If your main goal is to refresh an otherwise well-kept exterior, a maintenance wash may be enough.

Consider it between more comprehensive exterior details or after routine driving when you want the vehicle cleaned without adding the Full Exterior protection package. The right choice depends on how dirty the vehicle is, not just its age or size.

Point out heavy mud, sticky deposits, persistent water spots or dull paint before booking. These are different concerns from ordinary surface dirt. Full Exterior adds care and protection, while clay treatment, water-spot removal and polishing address their own specific needs.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose Full Exterior for wheel-well care and added paint and trim protection. Mini Exterior focuses on the wash and visible exterior surfaces.

  • Compare Full Exterior Detail

    Choose Full Exterior when you also want wheel-well cleaning, sealant and UV dressing. Mini is the less expensive, shorter option when a light wash is enough.

  • Compare Water Spot Removal - Paint

    Add paint water-spot removal when mineral spotting remains after washing. A more expensive wash package alone does not replace a targeted treatment.
