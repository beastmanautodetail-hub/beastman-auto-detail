When to choose it

Consider this for interior hard surfaces with grime around vents, seams, cupholders and other touchpoints. Describe the specific areas so the work can be distinguished from general light wiping.

The service focuses on hard interior surfaces. It is not the same as shampooing fabric seats or extracting carpets, so select the treatment based on the material and the problem rather than assuming one method covers the entire cabin.

Full Interior already includes steam cleaning. If you are choosing that package, discuss any extra scope before adding overlapping work. Mention damaged trim, loose coverings or delicate aftermarket finishes so their condition can be reviewed first.
