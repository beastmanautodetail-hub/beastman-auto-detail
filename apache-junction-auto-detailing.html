01

Interior detailing

Work gear, outdoor trips, and everyday errands can push dirt into seat seams, mats, cupholders, and cargo areas. Interior detailing focuses on the spaces you use most. For heavier buildup, choose Full Interior and identify pet hair, spills, or deeply soiled materials when booking.

02

Exterior detailing

Loose dust and road film should be washed away carefully instead of wiped across the paint. Exterior detailing covers the body, wheels, tires, and glass, with Full Exterior available for deeper cleaning and added protection. Heavy mud or unusual contamination should be mentioned in advance.

03

Full detail: inside and out

When the cabin and paint both show the effects of regular use, a full detail handles them during one coordinated visit. Choose Both in booking and select the package combination that matches the condition. Estimate-required paint services remain subject to inspection.

LOCAL QUESTIONS

Before your Apache Junction detail.

Can you work at a property with a gravel drive?

Possibly, but the setup and nearby dust matter. Describe the surface and available paved or stable work area so we can confirm whether the location is suitable.

Can you detail a truck or SUV used on dusty roads?

Yes. Select the correct vehicle type and describe the buildup honestly. Heavy mud, embedded dirt, pet hair, or special concerns may affect the recommended package or add-ons.

Does Paint Correction have a set time or price?

No. Paint Correction requires an estimate because both pricing and service time depend on inspecting the paint condition. The booking flow keeps it separate from fixed-time services.
