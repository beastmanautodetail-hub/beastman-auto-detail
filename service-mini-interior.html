THE BEASTMAN SERVICE GUIDE

Mini Interior Detail

A light interior reset for vehicles in generally good condition.

What it entails

  • Light vacuum including cracks and crevices
  • Interior window cleaning
  • All hard surfaces wiped down
  • Light seat cleaning / leather upholstery
  • Light door-jamb wipe down

Remove personal belongings before your appointment so the cabin surfaces can be reached.

When to choose it

Look for light dust on the dashboard, a few crumbs in the seats, footprints on the mats and fingerprints on the glass. If the cabin is otherwise in good shape, those are maintenance needs rather than signs that every surface needs a deep clean.

This is a practical choice between Full Interior appointments or before giving someone a ride when you want the cabin refreshed. Choose by the actual condition, not simply how long it has been since the last detail: a lightly used car and a busy family vehicle can need very different levels of care.

If dirt is packed into crevices, mats need more attention, or seats have concentrated stains, describe those issues first. Full Interior or a targeted extraction add-on may be the better fit. Mini Interior is not intended to cover heavy pet hair or a heavily soiled cabin.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose Full Interior for a more comprehensive clean, including steam cleaning, floor mats and added attention to crevices. Mini Interior keeps the focus on light upkeep.

  • Compare Full Interior Detail

    Choose Full Interior when dirt has built up in mats, vents and crevices and you need more than light upkeep. Mini is the lower-cost, shorter appointment for a cabin already in good condition.

  • Compare Mini Interior + Exterior

    Choose the Mini bundle if the outside also needs a light clean. It combines both services in one visit at a lower price than buying them individually.
