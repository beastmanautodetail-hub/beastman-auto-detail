Motorcycle Exterior Detail

A careful exterior detail for your motorcycle.

What it entails

  • Wheel and tire cleaning / tire shine
  • Pre-wash
  • Bug removal
  • Snow foam hand wash
  • Exterior surface and glass cleaning

Discuss the finish, accessories and any areas requiring special care before the appointment.

When to choose it

Choose this for a motorcycle exterior that needs attention to everyday dirt, bugs and visible surfaces. Tell Matt about the bike type, finish and accessories so the cleaning scope can be discussed for that motorcycle.

This is the motorcycle-specific option rather than a car package applied to a smaller vehicle. Describe hard-to-reach areas, any unusually heavy buildup and surfaces you want particular attention paid to.

A wash is different from finish restoration or mechanical work. Mention corrosion, damaged paint or known component issues before the appointment. If the goal goes beyond cleaning, confirm that additional scope rather than assuming it is included.

Why choose this over another option?

This is the motorcycle-specific exterior package. Car and SUV packages are designed around different vehicle surfaces and interiors.

  • Compare Mini Exterior Detail

    The car Mini Exterior package is for car/SUV surfaces. Choose Motorcycle Exterior Detail for the motorcycle-specific scope and discuss its finish and accessories before work.
