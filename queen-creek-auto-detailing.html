LOCAL QUESTIONS

Before your Queen Creek detail.

Can I arrange a Queen Creek detail after a hiking weekend?

Yes. Choose a package for the vehicle’s current condition and mention trail dirt or a heavily used cargo area. Heavy buildup may need more than a Mini service.

Is paint correction included with my exterior detail?

No. Washing and package protection differ from correcting paint defects. Correction and ceramic coating require an inspection or quote so expectations match the finish.

What should I check with my Queen Creek HOA?

Ask whether mobile vehicle cleaning and the proposed parking location are permitted. Let us know about gates or access restrictions before the appointment.
