Engine Bay Detail

Safely removes built-up dust, dirt, grease, and grime from under the hood, then conditions plastic and rubber components for a fresh, detailed finish.

What it entails

  • Safely removes built-up dust, dirt, grease, and grime from under the hood, then conditions plastic and rubber components for a fresh, detailed finish.

Point out known leaks, damaged components or electrical concerns before work begins.

When to choose it

Choose this for visible dust, dirt and grime in the engine compartment when you want attention beyond the bodywork. It can be useful before showing the vehicle or when the under-hood area has been overlooked during normal cleaning.

The reason to add it is the area being cleaned: an exterior detail does not automatically include the engine bay. Tell Matt about accessories, modifications and any areas you want reviewed before the work starts.

Cleaning is not mechanical diagnosis or repair. Known leaks, loose parts, warning lights or damaged electrical components should be identified first. If the concern is a mechanical problem rather than appearance, that needs to be addressed separately.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose Engine Bay Detail for the engine compartment. Exterior wash packages focus on the vehicle exterior and are not a substitute for this area-specific add-on.

  • Compare Full Exterior Detail

    Choose Full Exterior for comprehensive care of the outside of the vehicle. Add Engine Bay Detail only when you also want the under-hood area cleaned; it serves a separate area.

  • Compare Mini Exterior Detail

    Choose Mini Exterior for light bodywork, wheel and glass upkeep. It does not replace the focused under-hood work of Engine Bay Detail.
