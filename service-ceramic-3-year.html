When to choose it

Consider this when you want the 3-year coating option and your paint goals fit the included enhancement. Think about both the finish you want before coating and how you intend to care for the vehicle afterward.

Tell Matt about existing paint defects, previous coatings and your usual washing routine. Those details help determine whether the included preparation matches the vehicle or whether more involved correction needs to be discussed.

The package name is not a substitute for an inspection or an explanation of aftercare. Coating does not remove existing scratches by itself. Choose standalone enhancement if you only want gloss improvement, or compare the 5-year package for its different coating option and included 2-step correction.

Why choose this over another option?

Choose this for the enhancement-and-protection package. The 5-year package includes 2-step paint correction and is the more intensive option.
