LOCAL QUESTIONS

Before your Tempe detail.

Can you detail my car at a Tempe apartment?

We can discuss it, but the property must allow the work and provide a suitable space. Share garage clearances, assigned-space limits, and access details before booking.

Can I use a metered downtown space for my detail?

Do not rely on a street space as the service location. Contact us to discuss an approved area with adequate working room and any applicable parking restrictions.

Is a full detail useful before selling my Tempe car?

It can help present a clean cabin and exterior. We will discuss existing wear and condition honestly; detailing does not guarantee a higher sale price or remove every defect.
